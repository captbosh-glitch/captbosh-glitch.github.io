01

Pharmacy Dashboard

One dashboard that each role arranges for itself

The problem

People in different pharmacy roles need to read the same data in different ways. A single fixed dashboard served nobody well.

What I did

I designed a dashboard people manage themselves: choose charts from a palette, arrange them, and remove what they don’t need, on any device. Rounds of Balsamiq wireframes worked out the mechanics; responsive designs in Sketch went to product stakeholders, then engineering.

What changed

A flexible framework instead of one-size-fits-all reporting: each role sees its own view of the data.

02

ECS Optimizer Tool

A multi-step database workflow, on one screen

The problem

Optimizing Enriched Channel Sales data meant editing MySQL records, publishing, then reloading Spotfire templates just to check the result. It was slow and error-prone, and new hires took weeks or months to learn it.

What I did

I put the whole workflow on one screen that hides the database work and lets people publish and reload data instantly. After rounds of wireframes and interactive concepts, it was built on a responsive framework with JavaScript and direct Spotfire integration.

What changed

Analysts could optimize and check data in one place, without touching the database, which made the job far easier to learn.

03

Inventory Analytics journey map

Finding the friction before fixing it

The problem

The inventory analytics process had friction, but nobody had mapped it end to end.

What I did

I interviewed team members from beginners to experts, turned their workflows into personas, and built a journey map of each phase: the tasks, the pain points and the moods.

What changed

Stakeholders got a clear picture of where to fix the process first.

04

Trade-show and brand graphics

Messages that work in a few seconds, across a crowded hall

The problem

At a trade show, tables and people block the bottom of every banner, and attendees give a booth a few seconds.

What I did

I kept each message short, placed it high on the canvas, and used bold, high-contrast art. I built the art in Illustrator and laid it out in InDesign for print.

What changed

A consistent set of banners and booth graphics that read from across the hall.
