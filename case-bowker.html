Identifier Services

An eCommerce store for first-time authors

The problem

Independent authors needed one place to buy ISBNs, register their book details and learn how to self-publish. The business depended on that store converting.

What I did

I was the UX designer and Drupal theme developer on an eight-person team. I designed the purchase flow around retail best practices, ran A/B tests that routed traffic to the best-performing layouts, and designed product pages that cross-sold related services. I also worked with marketing to carry the design into mobile and print campaigns.

What changed

The winning layouts scaled automatically, and cross-selling on product pages raised average order value.

02

Bookwire

Book search for beginners and librarians alike

The problem

Bookwire’s bibliographic database served everyone from casual readers to experienced librarians. It had to be simple for both.

What I did

I was the product designer on a four-person team. Research with librarians shaped a simple search with advanced filters, plus a companion app for looking things up in the stacks. I worked closely with engineering to keep the scope realistic, and mapped the experience across device sizes.

What changed

One search that works for a beginner, with the depth a librarian needs, on desktop or in the aisles.
