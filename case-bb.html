01

Low fidelity first

Getting the flow right before the look

The problem

“Get roasted, get better” only works if posting is fast and the feed is fun. The core loop had to be clear before anything got styled.

What I did

I worked out the flow in Miro, then explored it in Figma Make: sign in, choose a mode, log an activity, see the feed, manage your group.

What changed

Five screens that set the structure for everything after.

02

Fourteen screens in Figma

A loud, playful look for a roast app

The problem

Each activity needed its own way to log a moment, but one app had to feel consistent across all four.

What I did

I designed 14 screens in Figma, from sign-in and profile to golf, bowling, gym and nights-out modes, the live feed and groups. Claude built a first phase on React and Supabase, with sign-in, Gym mode and a live feed with comments.

What changed

A working app friends tested on their own phones, with the remaining modes designed and ready to build.
