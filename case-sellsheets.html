Dense content, made scannable

Value propositions and a clear call to action

The problem

A seminar leave-behind has to drive engagement and sales, from content that started out dense.

What I did

I mirrored each corporate website’s look and feel, and restructured the content into scannable sections with value propositions and calls to action. Photoshop for assets, Illustrator for vector graphics, InDesign for layout and print-ready files.

What changed

A consistent set of sheets that match each brand and read at a glance.
