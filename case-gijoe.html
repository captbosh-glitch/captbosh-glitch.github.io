File cards, not wiki pages

The format fans already know

The problem

Fan wikis bury the basics under pages of trivia and ads. I wanted the quick answer: who is this, and which side are they on?

What I did

I designed a dark, compact grid with a team tag on every card, a search box and team filters on three tabs. Opening a card shows a file card: rank, specialty, first comic and cartoon appearance, creators, equipment and a short bio.

What changed

Four hundred entries you can scan in seconds, on a phone or a desktop.
