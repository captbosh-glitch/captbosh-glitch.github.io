Footwear that celebrates Africa

A footwear brand dedicated to celebrating Africa needed help promoting its shoes. I designed a site around the brand’s vibrant palette that puts the products first.

Role

Client provided

Work

A storefront in the brand’s colors

Every shoe tells you where it comes from

The problem

The client needed help promoting a brand of footwear dedicated to celebrating Africa.

What I did

I listened to their needs and researched the competitive space. Then I moved through several rounds of prototypes quickly, built on the palette’s vibrant colors, keeping attention on the products.

What changed

Each shoe uses materials indigenous to parts of Africa, so each product page points out the countries they come from.
