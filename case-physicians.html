Physician’s Dashboard

A shared dashboard to help physicians, hospitals and clinics in one network coordinate patient care.

Role
Sole UX Designer
Partners
Product team; an advisory council of medical professionals
Tools
Figma
Users
Physicians, hospitals, clinics
01

A network view of patient care

Lists, live analytics and partner links, arranged around each provider

The problem

Physicians, hospitals and clinics in the same network had no shared view of patient care, which left gaps in coordination.

What I did

I worked with the product team and an advisory council of medical professionals. The design lets each provider build custom lists, see real-time analytics and link to partner networks based on how they work. I iterated in Figma through several rounds of feedback before handing off to engineering.

What changed

A dashboard the council shaped with me, ready for engineering, that fits real clinical workflows.
