A site that updates itself

Scores, records and trends, every 10 minutes

The problem

Bettors who play the opening period or quarter have to piece together records from box scores by hand.

What I did

I designed game, trend and team pages for each sport, built on a pipeline that pulls schedules and scores, grades every pick and rebuilds the site every 10 minutes.

What changed

A game page for every sport, with first-segment records and trends that stay current without anyone touching them.

02

Tools for the person who runs it

An admin, AI that reads odds sheets, and automatic odds

The problem

My brother was typing odds in by hand for every game, every day.

What I did

I designed an admin with a “what’s missing” list, then added AI that reads a photo of an odds sheet. Now odds publish automatically from a licensed feed, and an Odds page compares every sportsbook side by side with the best price highlighted.

What changed

Hand entry is the exception now. The site fills itself in, and the admin shows only what still needs a person.
