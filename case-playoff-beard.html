A pipeline turned news collector

The Run to Your Bets pipeline, pointed at headlines

The problem

Following one team means checking a dozen outlets, and the same story shows up over and over.

What I did

I reused the automated data pipeline from Run to Your Bets and pointed it at news. It gathers headlines from many outlets, groups repeat stories into one, tags each story by league and team, and publishes static pages on a schedule.

What changed

Six leagues and 185 pages that update every hour, with no one at the keyboard.
